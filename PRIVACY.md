# Privacy Policy – Toolbar Controls for Amazon Music

Effective date: October 5, 2026

Toolbar Controls for Amazon Music ("the extension") is a browser extension for
Firefox and Chrome that adds play/pause, next and previous controls for the
Amazon Music web player to the browser toolbar.

**In short: the extension does not collect, store, or share any personal data.**

## What the extension accesses

The extension runs only on the Amazon Music websites (music.amazon.com and its
regional versions, such as music.amazon.co.uk and music.amazon.de). On those
pages it reads:

- the title, artist, album and artwork address of the song that is currently
  loaded, and
- whether the song is playing or paused.

It uses this only to show the song in its toolbar button and pop-up panel, and
to press the web player's own play/pause, next and previous buttons when you
click the extension's controls.

This information stays inside your browser. It is held in memory only while
it's needed and is never saved, never sent to the developer, and never sent to
anyone else. To display album artwork, the pop-up loads the image directly
from Amazon's servers, the same image the Amazon Music page itself shows.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not collect personal information, browsing history, or usage data.
- It has no analytics, advertising, or tracking, and sets no cookies.
- It does not sell, transfer, or share any data with third parties.
- It does not run on any website other than the Amazon Music sites.
- It does not download or run code from the internet.

## Permissions

- **Access to the Amazon Music sites:** needed to read the current song and to
  press the player's buttons, as described above.
- **Scripting:** used to start the extension in Amazon Music tabs that were
  already open when it was installed or updated.

## Amazon

The extension is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Amazon. Your
use of Amazon Music itself is covered by Amazon's own privacy notice.
